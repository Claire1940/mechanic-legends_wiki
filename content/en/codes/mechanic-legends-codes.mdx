<Section icon={Target} title="How to Make Money Fast: Step-by-Step">

The fastest early-game route in Mechanic Legends combines free rewards with smart junkyard flipping.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Collect Your Free Cash">
    Join the official Roblox group for 5,000 cash, then sell your starter car at the dealer for about 25,000 cash.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Shop the Junkyard">
    Wait for the junkyard refresh (cars rotate roughly every 12 minutes) and compare purchase price against the listed **estimated repair cost** and market value.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Repair Efficiently">
    Bring the car to your garage, lift it, and inspect with your wrench. Polish damaged parts, recharge or swap the battery, and check the engine, intercooler, and exhaust manifold.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Value">
    Strip and repaint the car at the paint station, mount new rims and tires, and consider parts from Parts Direct for performance upgrades.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Sell for Profit">
    Sell at the dealer. Focus on cars where market value clearly exceeds purchase price plus repairs — the listed estimate helps you judge this before buying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Repair Cost Rule">
A car priced at 22,000 with 3,300 in estimated repairs leaves a thin margin. A 1,000-repair car is usually the better flip target even if it looks rougher on the surface.
</Callout>
