<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Repair Routine">

Follow this routine every session to keep your garage running at maximum efficiency from the moment you log in.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Prep Your Client">
    Lower rendering and cap the game at 30 FPS before long farming sessions. This keeps performance stable while you grind repairs for extended periods.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Clear the Job Queue">
    Accept customer jobs and complete each repair fully, start to finish. Never abandon a job halfway — only completed jobs pay out.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Fill the Cooldown">
    When jobs run out, switch immediately to junkyard flips: buy cheap cars, repair them, and prepare them for sale.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Sell at Auction">
    List your repaired junkyard cars at the auction and let auto sell handle the transactions while the job queue refills.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Scale Up">
    Reinvest profits into better inventory and keep the loop running. More capital means more junkyard flips and bigger auction margins.
  </Step>
</Steps>
