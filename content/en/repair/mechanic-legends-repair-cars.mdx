The loop is simple: accept a job, fix the damage, hand the car back, and get paid. What separates casual players from top earners is how tightly they chain these jobs together and what they do during downtime.

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Building a High-Profit Repair Routine">

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Set Up Your Garage Workflow">
    Before farming, optimize your setup. If you're running long sessions, reduce graphics strain — lower frame caps and disable rendering effects keep sessions smooth on weaker devices and let repairs continue uninterrupted.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Prioritize Customer Repair Jobs">
    Customer jobs are your baseline income. Take every available job, complete repairs fully, and immediately check for the next one. Completing jobs end-to-end, rather than abandoning half-fixed cars, keeps payouts consistent.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Fill Cooldowns With Junkyard Flips">
    When jobs hit zero and the ~3 minute cooldown starts, head to the junkyard. Buy a wrecked car, repair it, and prep it for sale instead of waiting idle.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Sell Through the Auction">
    List repaired junkyard cars at auction to convert them to cash quickly. Chaining flips with customer jobs maximizes the total money earned per hour.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Add Passive Income With Auto Drive">
    When you need a break, auto drive lets your character take a car out on the road and generate income in the background. It's slower than active work but far better than nothing.
  </Step>
</Steps>
