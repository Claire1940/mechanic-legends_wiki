<Section icon={DollarSign} title="Maximizing Cash: Filling the Cooldown Gap">

The biggest inefficiency in Mechanic Legends auto repair is idle time. When customer jobs run out and the queue refreshes, players who sit and wait lose money. The fix is to layer income streams so something is always producing.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="Repair-First Loop">
    - Prioritize **customer jobs** whenever available
    - Highest cash per minute
    - Turnaround is fast once you know the repair steps
  </Card>
  <Card title="Junkyard Flipping">
    - Buy damaged cars cheap from the junkyard
    - **Repair and resell** for profit margin
    - Perfect filler during job cooldowns
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auction Combo">
    - List flipped cars on the **auction house**
    - Sells run in the background
    - Stacks with junkyard flips for double income
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

The optimal rotation looks like this: run every available customer job first. When the queue hits zero, immediately switch to junkyard purchases and repairs, then queue auction sales. By the time your flips are listed, new customer jobs have typically refreshed, and you loop back.

| Time Block | Activity | Estimated Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 0-10 min | Complete all customer repair jobs | **Primary income** earned |
| 10-13 min | Job cooldown: junkyard purchases | Wrecks acquired at low cost |
| 13-20 min | Repair flips, list on auction | Cars converted to listings |
| 20+ min | New jobs refresh, resume loop | Income compounds per cycle |

<Callout type="tip" title="Efficiency Tip">
Even short test drives between jobs add up. If a customer vehicle is drivable, take it for a quick spin — driving generates passive cash while you wait on the next repair.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Repair Workflow">

Follow this sequence for every customer job to keep your completion time as low as possible. Speed is the single biggest factor in your hourly earnings.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Accept the Customer Job">
    Open the jobs menu and claim the next available customer repair. Prioritize jobs with higher payouts if multiple are queued.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Diagnose the Vehicle">
    Walk around the car and identify every damaged component. Missing parts early means redoing steps later.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Complete All Repairs">
    Work through each damaged part methodically. Full completion is required — partial repairs don't pay out.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Return the Car and Collect">
    Deliver the finished vehicle and accept payment, then immediately claim the next job in the queue.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Fill Cooldowns with Flips">
    When jobs run dry, buy junkyard wrecks, repair them, and sell at auction until new jobs refresh.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Habit Building">
Players who memorize the repair order for common damage patterns complete jobs noticeably faster. After a dozen repetitions, the workflow becomes muscle memory.
</Callout>
