Your garage is called the **workshop**, and it holds every station you need. Here is what each one does:

| Station | Purpose | When to Use |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| Lift (press E) | Raises the car for underbody work | Before removing parts |
| Battery Charger | Restores dead or low batteries | Battery below full charge |
| Ultrasonic Cleaner | Removes rust from metal parts | Rusty radiators, manifolds |
| Part Polisher | Brings parts to 100% condition | After cleaning, before reinstalling |
| Paint Booth | Strips old paint and applies new color | Before selling a flipped car |

<Section icon={Map} title="Car Repair Step-by-Step">

Every restoration in Mechanic Legends follows the same workflow. Master this sequence and you can turn any junker around in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Buy a Trashed Car">
    Visit the junkyard area and inspect the available cars. Check the price and condition percentage. Lower percentage cars cost less upfront but yield bigger payouts when fully restored.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Raise and Open the Hood">
    Drive into the workshop, press E to lift the car, then pop the hood. Equip your wrench to begin removing damaged components.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Repair Each Part">
    Remove the battery and charge it at the battery charger. Pull rusty parts like the radiator and run them through the ultrasonic cleaner, then finish them in the part polisher until they read 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reinstall and Test">
    Bolt every repaired part back into the car. Lower the lift and confirm the engine starts. If it does not, check for missing components like a wiring harness.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Repaint and Sell">
    Follow the GPS to the paint shop, strip the old rusted paint down to clean metal, drive into the paint booth, and pick any color. Then head to the used car dealership and sell to the buyer for your profit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Do Not Skip the Polisher">
A part that comes out of the ultrasonic cleaner is not done. Always run it through the part polisher to hit 100% condition, or the car's resale value will suffer.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Free Starter Car and Common Problems">

After your first sale, the dealership offers you a **free starter car**. The guide picked the C4, a solid daily driver despite the stock color. These free cars sometimes arrive with hidden issues you must diagnose before driving.

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Dead Battery | Engine will not crank | Charge at battery charger |
| Missing Wiring Harness | No power to anything | Order and install a new harness |
| Rusted Radiator | Overheating risk | Ultrasonic clean, then polish |
| Worn Intake Manifold | Poor engine performance | Remove, clean, polish, reinstall |
| Rusted Paint | Low resale value | Strip and repaint at paint shop |

<Callout type="warning" title="Check the Wiring Harness">
Some starter and junk cars will not start even after battery repairs. Open the order menu and check whether the wiring harness is missing, as nothing gets power without it.
</Callout>
