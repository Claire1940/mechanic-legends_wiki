<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step: Flipping a Junk Car">

Follow this exact loop every time you flip a car. Once you have it memorized, a full restoration run takes only a few minutes per vehicle.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Buy a Junk Car">
    Visit the sales area and pick a trashed car. Prioritize high-value, low-condition vehicles over cheap ones that are nearly fixed.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Diagnose in the Workshop">
    Drive to the garage, raise the car with E, and pop the hood. Identify dead parts: battery, radiator, wiring, manifold.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Restore Every Part">
    Charge the battery, run rusty parts through the ultrasonic cleaner, then the part polisher until each hits 100%. Reinstall everything.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Repaint the Body">
    Follow the GPS to the paint shop. Strip the old paint to remove rust, then repaint in the booth — any color works for resale.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Sell for Profit">
    Drive to the used car dealership and sell to the buyer. A $4,000 junker can sell for around $10,700 — roughly $6,700 profit per flip.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Don't Skip the Polisher">
The ultrasonic cleaner removes rust, but parts often need the polisher afterward to reach 100% condition. Selling with partially restored parts cuts directly into your profit.
</Callout>

</Section>

| Junk car flipping | Medium | Medium | $6,700+ per car | Scaling profit |
