<Section icon={Wrench} title="Getting Started: What Mechanic Legends Is About">

Mechanic Legends is a hero-driven game where you assemble a roster, invest resources into your favorite characters, and push through progressively harder content. The core loop is simple: clear stages, earn upgrade materials, strengthen your team, repeat. What separates a strong start from a slow one is not luck — it is how deliberately you spend your first few sessions.

New players typically fall into the same traps: spreading resources across too many units, skipping the tutorial rewards, or spending premium currency the moment it appears. This guide walks through a clean, efficient first week so you avoid those mistakes entirely.

<Callout type="tip" title="Before You Start">
Complete the full tutorial before making any decisions. Many systems — gear, skills, and team slots — unlock as you progress, and early choices feel very different once you can see the whole picture.
</Callout>

The first thing to internalize is that Mechanic Legends rewards **focus**. A mid-tier unit raised to a high level will outperform a scattered bench of half-built characters in almost every game mode. Commit to a core team early, and only expand once that core can clear your current progression wall.

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Early Progression: Step-by-Step First Week">

Your first week sets the pace for everything after it. Follow this order and you will reach the mid-game with resources to spare.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Finish the Tutorial and Claim Everything">
    Complete the opening campaign missions and claim every one-time reward the game hands out. These early grants of currency and materials are the foundation of your first real upgrades.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Identify Your Core Team">
    Pick a main damage dealer, one support, and one flexible third slot based on the units the game gives you. Do not worry about tier lists yet — early content is clearable with almost any focused trio.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Push Campaign Until You Stall">
    Progress through the campaign until you hit a wall. The stage where you get stuck is your farm target for the next few days. Repeat it for materials instead of randomly grinding old stages.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Unlock and Understand Daily Systems">
    Daily missions, login rewards, and any idle or sweep mechanics become available as you advance. Enable all of them and build a short daily routine around them — this is where most free progression lives.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Join a Guild or Team Early">
    Social systems usually grant bonus currency, shared buffs, or extra shop access. Joining early costs nothing and compounds rewards over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Do Not Rush Rerolling">
Rerolling only makes sense if the game offers very limited top-tier units at launch. Hours spent rerolling are hours not spent learning systems. A playable start with early progress beats a "perfect" start with none.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Swords} title="Team Building Fundamentals">

A balanced team in Mechanic Legends covers three roles. Beginners often stack three damage dealers and wonder why they lose longer fights — survivability and utility win sustained battles.

<CardGrid cols={3}>
  <Card title="Damage Dealer">
    - **Primary kill source**
    - Prioritize highest upgrade priority
    - Usually benefits most from gear investment
  </Card>
  <Card title="Support / Healer">
    - Extends fight duration
    - Enables aggressive play
    - Scales well with modest investment
  </Card>
  <Card title="Flexible Third">
    - Tank, buffer, or second DPS
    - Adapt to the content you are stuck on
    - Rotate based on enemy composition
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

When evaluating which units to raise, use this comparison framework:

| Criteria | What to Look For | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Role coverage | Fills a gap your team lacks | High |
| Skill scaling | Stays useful into late game | High |
| Farmability | Obtainable without premium currency | Medium |
| Gear flexibility | Works with gear you already own | Medium |
| Personal preference | You enjoy playing them | High |

<Callout type="success" title="The Preference Rule">
A unit you enjoy playing will get played more, and time invested beats marginal stat differences in the early and mid game. Tier lists matter at the top end — not in week one.
</Callout>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Resource Management for New Players">

Resources are the real currency of progress in Mechanic Legends. Mismanaging them in week one can set you back weeks.

| Resource | Smart Usage | Common Mistake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Premium currency | Save for high-value limited offers or guaranteed pulls | Spending on random low-value refreshes |
| Upgrade materials | Concentrate on your core team | Splitting across the whole roster |
| Gear / equipment | Equip your main DPS first, then support | Leveling gear you will replace soon |
| Stamina / energy | Spend daily, target your current wall stage | Hoarding past the cap and wasting regen |
| Friendship / social points | Claim and spend every day | Ignoring social systems entirely |

The single most important rule: **never spread upgrades evenly**. A concentrated team of three strong units clears content that a bench of six average units cannot.

<Callout type="tip" title="Set a Spending Rule">
Before touching premium currency, decide on one rule — for example, "only spend on guaranteed outcomes." Write it down. Impulse pulls are the number one regret reported by players who restart.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Trophy} title="Daily Routine and First-Month Goals">

Consistency beats intensity. A fifteen-minute daily routine outperforms a single marathon session each week.

<Callout type="info" title="Sample Daily Routine">
1. Claim login and idle rewards. 2. Spend all stamina on your current farm stage. 3. Complete daily missions for bonus currency. 4. Do one arena or PvP attempt set. 5. Check the shop for free or currency-priced items. Total time: roughly 10–15 minutes.
</Callout>
