<Section icon={Coins} title="Where to Get Cars: Dealer, Junkyard, and Auction">

There are three main ways to acquire vehicles, each with different risk and reward profiles.

| Source | Cost | Risk | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dealership | Fixed, higher price | None | Cars you want to keep and drive |
| Junkyard | Low, varies per car | Medium | Flipping for profit |
| Garage Auction (mystery car) | One-time fee | High | Gamblers chasing rare cars |

The junkyard is the flipper's bread and butter. It refreshes on a timer (roughly every 12 minutes based on in-game countdown), so check back regularly for fresh stock.

<Callout type="warning" title="Check the Repair Estimate">
Before buying any junkyard car, compare the purchase price plus the **estimated repair cost** against the market value. A 22,000 car with 3,300 in repairs often barely breaks even — a 1,000-repair car is usually the smarter buy.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Flipping a Car for Profit">

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Buy Low-Repair Stock">
    Visit the junkyard and pick a car where the estimated repair cost is small relative to its market value. Interact with it (hold E) to see damage, condition, and repair estimates before committing.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Inspect on the Lift">
    Drive the car into your garage, lift it up, and open the hood and trunk. Use your wrench to hover over each part — battery, radiator, intercooler, exhaust manifold — and see what needs work.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Repair and Polish">
    Charge or swap the battery, polish damaged parts like the intercooler and exhaust manifold back to 100%, and reinstall everything. Super-clean parts for maximum condition.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Tires and Mods">
    Use the splitter and mounting machine to swap tires and rims. Visit Parts Direct for engine upgrades and tuning parts — modifications can push performance past stock limits.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Paint and Sell">
    Strip the old paint, apply a fresh color at the paint station, then sell at the sales point. A clean respray makes the car far more attractive to buyers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Investment Mindset">
Paint stripping and repairs cost real money up front. Treat every flip as an investment — the sale price should recover your costs plus profit. Skip cars where the math doesn't work.
</Callout>

</Section>
