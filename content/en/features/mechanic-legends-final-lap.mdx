The **Mechanic Legends Final Lap** arc turns a quiet repair shop into the center of the racing world. Joe, a mechanic who has spent five years running errands for the Langford family, is pushed into the spotlight when the Flying Riders, the third-ranked pro team in the country, scheme to seize the resort's private mountain road through a staged repair scam.

When negotiation fails, the conflict is settled the only way racers know: a best-of-three showdown on the mountain itself. Joe's impossible driving — full-throttle bottle runs, a flawless three-wheel egg course, and a final-lap overtake against national pro Chris Ryder — reveals that the "grease monkey" is anything but ordinary.

<Section icon={Trophy} title="The Tragedy Behind the Retirement">

Joe's refusal to race again traces back to a devastating accident: he and his younger brother were racing head-to-head when his brother pushed too hard into a curve, spun out, and was killed in the collision. Joe blames himself and quit the sport entirely, returning only to honor his brother's dream of winning the Serial trophy.

This is why Joe coaches instead of competes. He secures three referral slots from Alan, trains the Langford siblings and Daniel on simulator maps, and takes the head coach position for Alan's new national team — the same role the Flying Riders sneered at him for.
