Vehicle customization is the heart of Mechanic Legends. Every car you acquire can be stripped down, restored, rebuilt, and tuned to match your playstyle. <Section icon={Settings} title="Part Upgrade Priorities: What to Buy First">

Not every part gives equal value for your currency. Testing different loadouts shows a clear priority order for players who want to win events without overspending.

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid Early Cosmetic Spending">
Paint and livery options are tempting, but they add no performance. Buying cosmetics before completing mechanical upgrades usually leaves you underpowered for the events that fund your garage.
</Callout>

**Recommended upgrade order:**

| Priority | Part Group | Why It Comes First | Est. Performance Gain |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Engine internals | Base power drives all other stats | **High** |
| 2 | Turbo / intake | Multiplies existing engine power | High |
| 3 | Gearbox | Converts power into usable speed | High |
| 4 | Tires & brakes | Cornering and control | Medium |
| 5 | Suspension | Stability at high speed | Medium |
| 6 | Weight reduction | Improves every stat slightly | Medium |
| 7 | Cosmetics | Style points only | Low |

<Callout type="success" title="The 60/30/10 Rule">
A reliable budget split for any project car: **60% engine and drivetrain**, **30% handling parts**, **10% styling**. This keeps builds competitive while still looking the part.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Customization Workflow">

Follow this workflow for every vehicle project to avoid wasted parts and currency:

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Full Teardown & Inspection">
    Strip the vehicle completely and inspect every component. Catalog damaged or worn parts so nothing gets missed during the rebuild.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Set Your Build Goal">
    Decide the purpose before buying: race performance, resale restoration, or show styling. This decision dictates every purchase that follows.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Install Core Mechanical Parts">
    Start with the engine, gearbox, and drivetrain. Match part quality across the group so no single weak component bottlenecks the build.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Tune Suspension & Brakes">
    Match tires, brakes, and suspension to the engine output. A powerful engine with stock brakes loses time in every corner.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Finish With Visual Styling">
    Apply paint, rims, and interior touches last, once the performance layer is locked in and tested.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Test Between Stages">
Run a test event after each major stage. This isolates which upgrades actually improved your times and helps you fine-tune the next purchase.
</Callout>

</Section>
