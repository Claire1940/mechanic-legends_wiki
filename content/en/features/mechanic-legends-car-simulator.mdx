<Section icon={Map} title="What Is Mechanic Legends Car Simulator?">

Mechanic Legends car simulator is a Roblox vehicle experience developed by Mechanic Devs, originally known as Mecánica Argentina. The game covers the full garage lifestyle: repairing and dismantling engines, customizing and tuning cars, and taking your builds to the street for drag races. With more than 31.9 million visits, 50+ collectible cars, and roughly 99% English translation, it has grown from a Spanish-language project into a game accessible to a global audience.

You can play it directly through the [official Roblox game page](https://www.roblox.com/games/9265443535/Car-Mechanic-Simulator), and the community gathers in the official [Discord server](https://discord.gg/mecanicaarg).

<Section icon={Wrench} title="Tuning and Engine Work: Step-by-Step">

Performance comes from the parts you install and how you balance acceleration, speed, and handling. Work through the workshop systematically instead of randomly swapping components.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Inspect before changing parts">
    Identify damaged, missing, or low-performance components so repairs stay separate from upgrades.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Remove and replace engine components">
    Handle engine work one component at a time until the engine is restored or upgraded.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Install racing-oriented parts">
    Choose parts that match your intended race type, prioritizing engine upgrades for straight-line speed.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Balance the setup">
    Speed-focused for drag racing, acceleration-focused for launches, handling-focused for drift and corners.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Test and refine">
    Change one group of parts at a time so each adjustment's effect stays clear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Match the Build to the Activity">
A fast car is not always the quickest car on every route. Long straights reward top speed; technical roads reward control and predictable handling.
</Callout>

</Section>
