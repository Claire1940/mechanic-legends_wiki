<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Upgrade Workflow">

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Diagnose the Car">
    Raise the car, open the hood, and review the damage list. Note which parts are dirty (cleanable), damaged (repairable), or scrapped (must be replaced).
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Clean and Charge">
    Remove the battery and charge it at the charger. Clean the radiator and polish serviceable parts while you wait — better-condition parts mean a higher final value.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Order Replacement Parts">
    Head to the Parts Store and order what's scrapped, such as an engine block or exhaust manifold. Buy your performance upgrades — engine and tuning mods — in the same trip.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reinstall and Upgrade">
    Install the new parts, wiring harness, and upgraded engine components. Close the hood and lower the car, then confirm it starts.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Sell or Keep">
    Drive to the dealership to sell for market value, or keep the car. Reinvest the profit into a bigger junkyard bargain with more upgrade potential.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Batch Your Trips">
Order all parts at once. Delivery takes time — use the wait to polish other components or take a repair job for extra income.
</Callout>
