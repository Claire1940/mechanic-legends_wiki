<Section icon={Flame} title="Temperature Management: The 160° Rule">

Overheating is the single biggest threat to any build in Mechanic Legends. Every risky drive is a gamble with your total investment.

| Temperature Range | Status | Recommended Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 90° and below | Safe | Normal driving |
| 100-130° | Warning | Ease off throttle, avoid hills |
| 130-155° | Critical | Stop and let it cool |
| 160° | **Explosion** | Car catches fire, total loss |

Without a radiator, even moderate acceleration sends temps soaring. Installing one costs 20,000 coins, which is only worthwhile on keeper cars you plan to drive hard, not on flips you are selling immediately.

<Callout type="warning" title="Uphill Acceleration Is a Trap">
Climbing hills at full throttle without a radiator spikes temperature faster than flat-road driving. If you must climb a bridge or incline, feather the throttle and accept the slower speed.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Zap} title="Turbines and Engine Swaps: Costly Lessons">

The turbine is the marquee performance upgrade, but its behavior surprises many players. In testing, a turbocharged 4-cylinder Pojo launched dramatically harder off the line, yet its top speed stayed at **196 km/h**, just short of the 200 mark.

Swapping the engine is even more consequential. Replacing the 4-cylinder with a V6 **removed every part installed on the previous engine**, including the turbine, forcing a full repurchase of components. Budget accordingly before committing to an engine swap.
