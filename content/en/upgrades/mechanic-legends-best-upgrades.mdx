<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step: Upgrading Your First Car">

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Diagnose the Car">
    Raise the vehicle, open the hood, and inspect every part. Identify which components are dirty, damaged, or missing entirely.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Charge and Clean">
    Pull the battery and place it on the charger. While it charges, wash and polish dirty parts like the radiator so everything is in top condition before reassembly.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Remove Damaged Components">
    Use your wrench to extract broken parts. For engine work, remove the exhaust manifold and related components first so the engine block can come out.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Order Replacements">
    Visit the parts store and buy what you need — wiring, engine blocks, manifolds, or better engines. It costs money up front, but it's an investment in the resale price.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Reassemble, Paint, and Sell">
    Install all new parts, close the hood, lower the car, and optionally repaint at the paint shop. Drive to the dealership and sell for profit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Double Your Efficiency">
While waiting for ordered parts to arrive, polish every remaining component. You are idle anyway — use that time to push each part toward perfect condition.
</Callout>

</Section>
