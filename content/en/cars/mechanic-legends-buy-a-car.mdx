<Section icon={Wrench} title="From Purchase to Profit: The Full Flip Cycle">

Once you buy a car in Mechanic Legends, the goal is to restore every component to 100% and maximize its resale value. Follow this proven cycle:

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Buy a Trashed Car">
    Visit the dealership or map and look for low-percentage cars with high price tags, one example was a car valued around $41,000. Low condition means a bigger margin after restoration.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Repair in the Workshop">
    Drive into the garage, raise the car with E, and open the hood. Use your wrench to remove damaged parts. Charge dead batteries at the battery charger, and clean rusty parts in the ultrasonic cleaner.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Polish Every Part">
    Run cleaned components through the part polisher to bring them to 100%. A polished radiator or intake manifold restores full vehicle value.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reinstall and Repaint">
    Bolt everything back in, then follow the GPS to the paint shop. Strip the old paint to remove rust, then use the paint booth to apply any color you want.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Sell at the Dealership">
    Drive the restored car to the used car dealer and negotiate. A fully restored flip sold for $10,700, roughly $6,700 over the purchase price.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Profit Benchmark">
A single junk-to-gem flip can net around $6,700. Repeat the cycle on higher-value cars to scale your income fast.
</Callout>

</Section>
