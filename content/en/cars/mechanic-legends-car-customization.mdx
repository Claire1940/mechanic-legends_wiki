<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Customization Workflow">

Follow this workflow to take a stock vehicle from basic frame to fully customized machine. The sequence mirrors how the Mechanic Legends team approaches every project: foundation first, technology second, innovation last.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Establish the Chassis">
    Select your base frame and reinforce the chassis. Ryan Walker's mechanical expertise makes this the natural starting point — every later upgrade depends on a solid structure.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Install the Powertrain">
    Choose between a conventional high-output engine or a Lexi-designed clean energy cell. Match the powertrain to your intended build path from the previous section.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Integrate Smart Systems">
    Add Hela's AI modules for navigation, hazard detection, and automated responses. These systems work best when the mechanical foundation is already stable.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Tune and Iterate">
    Test the vehicle under real conditions, log weaknesses, and return to the garage. Ryan's philosophy of building, repairing, and improving means customization is never truly finished.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Team Review">
    Share your build with teammates. Great teamwork achieves incredible things — a second pair of eyes often catches flaws a solo builder misses.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Iterate Like the Legends">
Hela never gives up until the mission is complete, and neither should your build process. Plan at least two tuning cycles after your initial customization before calling a project done.
</Callout>

</Section>
