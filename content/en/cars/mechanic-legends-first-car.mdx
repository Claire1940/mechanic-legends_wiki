<Section icon={Wrench} title="Repairing Your First Car Step-by-Step">

Once you own a car, drive it back to your garage. The repair loop in Mechanic Legends is hands-on: you physically open the hood, pull the engine parts out with your wrench, run them through the part repairing machine, and reinstall them.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Drive Home and Park">
    Follow the emblems in the sky toward your garage. Park inside and open the hood. Note that a car lacking a radiator will heat up fast on the drive back.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Pull Engine Parts">
    Grab your wrench tool and remove every engine part. Move the parts close to the part repairing machine to save travel time on multiple trips.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Repair Each Part">
    Place each part into the machine. Parts below 100% health get restored. Electronics cannot be repaired — those must be purchased new at the engine shop.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Reinstall and Close Up">
    Put every repaired part back into the engine bay, close the hood, and take the driver's seat. The car should run noticeably better.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Efficiency Tip">
Move the whole car as close to the repairing machine as possible before dismantling. Fewer trips between the vehicle and the machine cuts your repair time dramatically.
</Callout>

Engine matching is critical. Before buying replacement parts, confirm which engine type your car uses — an i4 car cannot accept a V8 block, and buying the wrong engine is money straight down the drain.

| Part | Approx. Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radiator | **$20,000** | Keeps engine below 160°C, required for long drives |
| i4 engine block | $9,000 | Must match your car's engine type |
| Full engine (wrong type example) | $35,000 | V8 won't fit an i4 car — always check first |
| Paint job | ~$113,000 | Premium option, dramatically raises resale value |
| Race wheel | $86,000 | Skip on early flips, rarely pays off |

</Section>

<Section icon={Target} title="Common First-Car Mistakes">

New players lose money in predictable ways. Avoid these and your early profits compound quickly:

- **Buying the wrong engine type** — always confirm i4 vs V8 before purchasing
- **Skipping the radiator on long drives** — overheating at 160°C destroys the car
- **Repairing parts already at 100% health** — check part health first
- **Spending on paint for cheap cars** — cosmetics rarely return their cost on budget flips
- **Forgetting code cash** — redeem available codes before your first junkyard visit
