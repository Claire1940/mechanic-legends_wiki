<Section icon={Target} title="Step-by-Step Auction Flip Workflow">

Follow this workflow every session to keep your cash flowing even when repair jobs dry up.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Stock Up on Jobs">
    Start with customer repair jobs, since they pay the fastest. Keep working them until the queue hits zero and the cooldown begins.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Switch to the Auction">
    During the roughly 3-minute cooldown, head to the auction. Look for undervalued cars you can buy below their repaired resale price.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Repair and Resell">
    Fix every purchased car before selling. The margin between auction price and repaired sale price is your profit, so repair quality matters.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Add Junkyard Flips">
    Between auctions, flip junkyard cars. They are cheap to acquire, and after repairs they still sell for a solid margin.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Return to Jobs">
    Once the cooldown ends, jump back to customer jobs and repeat the cycle. The loop keeps every income stream active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="Avoid This Mistake">
Do not sell cars unrepaired. The resale margin after repair is the entire point of auction flipping — skipping repairs destroys your profit.
</Callout>

</Section>

<Section icon={Shield} title="Profit Expectations and Pitfalls">

Auction profits scale with two factors: how cheaply you buy and how quickly you repair. New players often overpay for auction cars, leaving no margin after repairs. Set a personal buy ceiling — if a car's auction price is close to its repaired sale value, skip it and check the junkyard instead.

| Pitfall | Consequence | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overbidding at auction | No profit margin | Compare prices before bidding |
| Selling unrepaired cars | Lost repair value | Always repair first |
| Idling during cooldowns | Wasted earning time | Run auction or junkyard flips |
| Ignoring junkyard | Missing cheap inventory | Check junkyard between auctions |

<Callout type="tip" title="Track Your Margins">
Keep a simple mental note of what each car type sells for once repaired. Knowing these numbers lets you spot underpriced auction lots instantly.
</Callout>
