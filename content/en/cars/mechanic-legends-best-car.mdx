<Section icon={Map} title="Step-by-Step Car Restoration Workflow">

Every successful flip follows the same workshop sequence. Master this workflow and you can restore almost any junker to 100% condition.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Buy and Position the Car">
    Purchase a trashed car and drive it into the workshop garage. Raise it on the lift by pressing E, then pop the hood to inspect the engine bay.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Diagnose and Repair Core Parts">
    Check the battery at the charger, remove rusted parts like the radiator, run them through the ultrasonic cleaner, then finish each component in the part polisher until it reads 100%.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Reinstall and Test">
    Bolt the restored parts back into the car, confirm the engine is good, then lower the vehicle off the lift.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Repaint at the Paint Shop">
    Follow the GPS to the paint shop, strip the old rusted paint down to clean metal, then pull into the paint booth and apply any color you want.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Sell at the Used Car Dealership">
    Drive the fully restored car to the used car dealer and sell it. Well-executed flips can return $10,700 or more on a single sale.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Workflow Shortcut">
Both the ultrasonic cleaner and part polisher are inside the workshop. Route parts through cleaner first, then polisher — this two-step process is what pushes components to the full 100% condition.
</Callout>

</Section>
