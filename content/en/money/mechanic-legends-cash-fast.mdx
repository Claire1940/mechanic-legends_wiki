<Section icon={Map} title="Car Flipping: Step-by-Step Profit Loop">

Car flipping is the backbone of making cash fast in Mechanic Legends. Follow this loop every time to maximize your return on every junker you buy.

<Steps>
  <Step num="1" title="Buy a Trashed Car">
    Purchase a junk car from the dealership. Prioritize vehicles with low condition percentages and higher listed values — a car worth around $41,000 can return strong profits once restored. Don't forget to claim your free starter car at the dealership.
  </Step>
  <Step num="2" title="Diagnose in the Workshop">
    Drive into the garage workshop, raise the car by pressing E, and pop the hood. Equip the wrench and check each component — dead batteries, rusty radiators, and missing wiring harnesses are the usual suspects.
  </Step>
  <Step num="3" title="Restore Every Part to 100%">
    Charge dead batteries at the battery charger. Place rusted parts like radiators into the ultrasonic cleaner to strip rust, then finish them in the part polisher. A part must reach a fully polished state before you reinstall it.
  </Step>
  <Step num="4" title="Repaint at the Paint Shop">
    Follow the GPS to the paint shop. Strip the old paint to remove rust and expose clean metal, then drive into the paint booth and apply any color you want. A fresh coat noticeably increases the car's visual appeal and resale value.
  </Step>
  <Step num="5" title="Sell at the Used Car Dealership">
    Drive the restored car to the used car dealer and talk to the buyer to see the offer. A fully restored flip can net around $6,700 in profit — then immediately reinvest in your next junker.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="success" title="Reinvest Every Dollar">
Roll your profit directly into the next, more expensive junk car. Each cycle increases your buying power, and higher-priced wrecks carry higher absolute profits.
</Callout>

</Section>

</Callout>

Customer jobs are optional — if you prefer, you can skip them entirely and focus purely on flipping cars from the lot. The most efficient players blend both: run an easy job while deciding which junk car to buy next.

<Callout type="tip" title="Pick Jobs Strategically">
Choose the jobs that pay the most relative to the number of tasks. A $1,900 job repairing one intake manifold and one wiring harness beats a longer job with double the work for marginally more money.
</Callout>
